# SYNQ Marketing — Launch Playbook (Rockford, IL)

Goal: 3 paying clients in 30 days. Everything below is ready to use.

## 1. Setup (day 1, under $15)

| Task | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Buy `synqmarketing.com` (was unregistered when checked on 2026-11-21; buy soon) | ~$12/yr | You |
| Publish the site in `marketing-agency/` (Netlify: new site, publish directory `marketing-agency`) | $0 | You, ~5 min |
| Create Instagram + Facebook page "SYNQ Marketing" | $0 | You |
| Google Business profile (service-area business, Rockford) | $0 | You |
| Canva free, CapCut, Meta Business Suite | $0 | You |

The contact form uses the same Web3Forms key as the studio site, so leads land in the same inbox with subject "New SYNQ Marketing lead".

## 2. Who to contact first

Best first clients: busy owner, active business, weak online presence.

- Restaurants, taquerías, food trucks, bakeries
- Barbershops, salons, nail and lash studios
- Auto repair, detailing, tire shops
- Contractors: roofing, landscaping, cleaning, HVAC
- Gyms, boxing, dance studios

How to find them (free): Google Maps → search "barbershop Rockford IL" → open each → look for:
- Instagram/Facebook last post over a month ago
- Unanswered Google reviews
- Few or old photos, no website

Track every business in a sheet: `Name | Type | Phone | Instagram | Problem seen | Contacted date | Reply | Next step`.

Target: 15 contacts a day, Mon–Fri.

## 3. Scripts

### Instagram / Facebook DM
> Hey [Name]! I'm [You], I run SYNQ Marketing here in Rockford. I was checking out [Business] — [specific compliment, e.g. "those birria tacos look crazy"]. I noticed your page hasn't posted since [month]. I'm taking on 3 local businesses this month at half price for the first month to run their posts and Google reviews. Want me to send you a free 3-point audit of your page? No strings.

### Spanish DM
> ¡Hola [Nombre]! Soy [Tú], tengo SYNQ Marketing aquí en Rockford. Vi la página de [Negocio] y [cumplido]. Me di cuenta que no han publicado desde [mes]. Este mes estoy aceptando 3 negocios locales con el primer mes a mitad de precio para manejar sus publicaciones y reseñas de Google. ¿Le mando una revisión gratis de su página? Sin compromiso.

### Walk-in (best conversion — go at slow hours, 2–4pm)
> Hi, is the owner around? … I'm [You], I do marketing for local businesses here in Rockford. I'm not here to sell you anything today — I put together a quick free review of your Google and Instagram. Can I leave it with you, and text you Thursday to see what you think?

Bring a printed one-page audit (section 4).

### Follow-up text (2–3 days later)
> Hey [Name], it's [You] from SYNQ Marketing. Did you get a chance to look at the audit? Happy to show you what your first month of posts would look like — takes 10 minutes.

### Handling "how much?"
> Most businesses start at $600 a month, which covers posts, your Google profile and reviews. For the first three clients the first month is $300. Month to month, cancel any time.

### Handling "I'll think about it"
> Totally fair. Want me to make you 3 sample posts for free so you can see the quality first?

## 4. Free audit template (send me the business name; I'll fill it in)

**[Business] — Marketing Audit by SYNQ Marketing**
1. **Google profile:** [rating, # reviews, unanswered reviews, photos, hours accuracy] → Fix: …
2. **Social media:** [last post date, posting frequency, bio, link] → Fix: …
3. **Website / ordering:** [exists? mobile? speed? clear call to action?] → Fix: …

**The 3 changes we'd make first:** 1. … 2. … 3. …
**What month one looks like:** 16 posts, all reviews answered, 1 promo.

Only use facts you can see on their public pages. Never invent numbers.

## 5. When someone says yes

1. Send a simple agreement (I'll draft one: services, price, month to month, 30 days' notice, client owns their content, client pays ad spend).
2. Get paid before starting: Square invoice / Zelle / Cash App. Monthly, upfront.
3. Onboarding: logins (ask them to add you as a page admin in Meta Business Suite; never ask for their password), logo, photos, specials, busy/slow days.
4. Send me the onboarding info → I produce the month's calendar: posts, captions, graphics briefs, video scripts, review replies.
5. Client approves → you schedule in Meta Business Suite.
6. End of month → I write the report from the numbers you pull from Meta and Google.

## 6. Rules

- Never promise sales numbers.
- Never post fake reviews or buy followers.
- Don't use music or images you don't have rights to (Canva/CapCut libraries are fine within their licenses).
- Client approves every post.
- Keep client logins in a password manager, never in chat or a shared doc.
