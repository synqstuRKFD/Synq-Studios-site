# SYNQ Clips — Clipping Workflow (computer)

Goal: turn one long video (podcast, stream, interview, studio session) into 5–10 short vertical clips in about 1–2 hours.

## Free tool kit

| Tool | Use | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **CapCut Desktop** | Main editor: auto-captions, 9:16 reframe, cuts, zoom | Free (some effects/features are Pro; you don't need them) |
| **OpusClip** (optional) | AI picks highlight moments and makes first-draft clips | Free tier with limited minutes; paid plans exist |
| **DaVinci Resolve** (optional) | Heavier editing if CapCut isn't enough | Free |
| **Google Drive** | Receive files from clients, deliver finished clips | Free (15 GB) |
| **Google Sheets** | Track clips, posts, views, payouts | Free |
| **Claude (me)** | Pick moments from transcripts, write hooks, titles, captions, hashtags | Your Claude plan |

Only clip videos you have permission to use: a campaign that grants it, a client who hired you, or your own content. Never download and repost someone's content without permission.

## The workflow

### 1. Get the source (5 min)
- Campaign: download the official files/links the campaign provides.
- Client: they share a Google Drive folder.
- Make a folder per video: `YYYY-MM-DD_source-name/` with `raw/`, `clips/`, `final/`.

### 2. Get a transcript (5 min)
- CapCut: import → Captions → Auto captions → export the captions as text (or copy them).
- Or upload to OpusClip, which transcribes automatically.
- Paste the transcript to me with: "Find the 8 best clip moments for TikTok from this."

### 3. Pick moments (me, 2 min)
I return for each clip: start/end timestamp, hook line, why it works, title, caption, hashtags.
Good clip moments:
- A strong opinion or hot take
- A story with a payoff in under 60 seconds
- A surprising fact or number
- A funny or emotional reaction
- A clear "how to" tip
- A disagreement between two people

### 4. Cut (10–15 min per clip)
In CapCut:
1. New project → **9:16** canvas.
2. Drop in the source, trim to the timestamps. **Cut the first 1–2 seconds of dead air.** The first spoken word should be the hook.
3. Reframe so the speaker's face fills the frame. For two people, split screen top/bottom or cut between them.
4. **Auto captions**: big, bold, 2–4 words per line, center-lower third. Highlight key words in a color.
5. Add a **hook text** at the top for the first 2–3 seconds (from the hook pack).
6. Tighten: delete pauses, "um"s, and anything that doesn't move the story.
7. Optional: subtle zoom-ins on punchlines, a sound effect on a key moment. Use only CapCut's licensed sounds.
8. Target length: **15–45 seconds** for campaigns, up to 60–90 for story clips.
9. Export **1080×1920, 30 fps**.

### 5. Quality check (1 min per clip)
- [ ] Hook in the first 2 seconds
- [ ] Captions match what's said (fix names and slang)
- [ ] No black bars, face centered
- [ ] Audio clear, no clipping
- [ ] Ends on a strong line, not mid-sentence
- [ ] Follows campaign rules (required tags, links, disclosure)

### 6. Post (campaigns) or deliver (clients)
- Campaigns: post from accounts that meet the campaign's rules, add the required tags/disclosure, submit the post link in the campaign dashboard.
- Clients: upload to their `final/` folder with a sheet listing title, caption and hashtags for each clip.
- Best posting times to test: 11am–1pm and 6–9pm local. Measure, then adjust.

### 7. Track (2 min a day)
Sheet columns: `date | source | clip title | platform | post link | views 24h | views 7d | payout | notes`.
Every week, send me the sheet: I'll tell you which hooks and topics are working and what to make more of.

## Account setup (for campaigns)
- Make **dedicated accounts** per niche on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube (e.g. a podcast-clips page). Don't mix with your personal account.
- Warm them up for a few days: complete the profile, follow and watch content in the niche, post 1–2 clips a day.
- Never buy views, followers or use view bots. Platforms detect it, and campaigns ban and don't pay for botted views.

## Daily routine (2–3 hours)
1. 30 min: pick sources / check campaign dashboards for new campaigns
2. 90 min: cut 4–6 clips
3. 15 min: post and submit links
4. 15 min: update the tracking sheet

## Time per clip goal
- Week 1: ~25 min per clip (learning)
- Week 3: ~10 min per clip (templates saved in CapCut)

Save a **CapCut template** once you have a caption style you like, so every clip looks consistent.
