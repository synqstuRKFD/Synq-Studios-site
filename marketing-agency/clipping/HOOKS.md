# SYNQ Clips — Hook & Caption Pack

The first 2 seconds decide whether someone keeps watching. Put a hook as on-screen text AND make sure the first spoken line is strong.

## On-screen hook templates (fill the blank)

**Curiosity**
- "He didn't expect this answer…"
- "Nobody talks about this part of ___"
- "Wait for the last line"
- "This is why ___ actually happened"
- "The ___ secret nobody tells you"

**Hot take / conflict**
- "This is going to make people mad"
- "He said what everyone's thinking"
- "Unpopular opinion: ___"
- "She shut him down in 5 seconds"
- "They completely disagreed on this"

**Story**
- "How he went from ___ to ___"
- "The moment everything changed"
- "He almost quit until ___"
- "This story is crazy"

**Value / how-to**
- "Do this if you want ___"
- "3 things I wish I knew about ___"
- "Stop doing ___"
- "The easiest way to ___"

**Numbers**
- "$___ in ___ days, here's how"
- "He turned down $___"
- "___ years in 30 seconds"

**Music / studio (for SYNQ content)**
- "How this beat was made in 10 minutes"
- "First take vs final take"
- "He wrote this verse on the spot"
- "Before vs after the mix"
- "Cómo se grabó este corrido"

Rule: the hook has to be **true to the clip**. Misleading hooks get swiped away, hurt the account, and can break campaign rules.

## Caption formula
`[Hook restated or a question] + [1 line of context] + [call to action] + [3–5 hashtags]`

Examples:
- "Would you take the deal? He turned down a record contract to stay independent. Comment what you'd do 👇 #musicbusiness #independentartist #podcast"
- "This advice hit different. Save it for later. #entrepreneur #mindset #podcastclips"
- "¿Tú qué harías? 👀 #corridos #regionalmexicano #musica"

## Hashtags
Use 3–5: 1–2 broad (#podcast, #fyp is optional and not magic), 2–3 niche (#musicproducer, #regionalmexicano, #smallbusiness). Campaigns may require specific tags; always add them.

## Caption style in CapCut (save as template)
- Font: bold sans (e.g. "Montserrat ExtraBold" or CapCut's "Bold")
- Size: large, 2–4 words per line
- Color: white with black outline; key words in yellow or orange
- Position: lower-middle, above the app buttons
- Animation: simple pop-in per word
